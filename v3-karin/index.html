Most of us live in our heads, breathing high in the chest, at someone else’s pace.

I’m Karin. For years I lived that way too, trying to fit inside the corporate world, until I finally listened. I trained in breathwork, Forest Therapy and coaching, and opened HJEM, our home by the sea, as a place to slow down.

LYS by Heart is my method of breath, movement and time in nature. It helps you slow down, feel your body again and return to your own rhythm.

Many of the people I work with are women with full working lives: leaders, therapists and creatives who carry a lot and rarely stop.

I can’t give you anything you don’t already have. I can help you remember it.

Karin

What to expect

LYS by Heart in short: what you do, where it happens and who it is for.

What you do

Breathe, move, go outside

Simple breathing practices, gentle movement and slow time in the forest and by the sea. You come away calmer, breathing more deeply and back in your own rhythm, with a few practices to keep using at home. No experience needed.

About the method

Where

At HJEM, in Brussels or online

HJEM is my home in Port-Blanc on the Pink Granite Coast of Brittany: three connected houses with a sauna, a rock garden and a long table. Gatherings also take place in Brussels, and one-to-one sessions online.

See HJEM

Who it’s for

For full working lives

For busy professionals, many of them women: leaders, therapists and creatives who feel stuck in their heads and want to feel their body again. I also host seminars for teams.
